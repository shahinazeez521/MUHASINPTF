Project Description

The Yas Waterworld extension features over 20 new rides, slides, and attractions in a "Lost City" theme, adding to the park's existing offerings. The expansion includes a new dining experience, a new theme based on UAE pearl diving heritage, and is designed to appeal to all ages and family members
