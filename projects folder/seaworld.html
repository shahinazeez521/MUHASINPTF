01 — Design Development From concept to construction

Translate design concepts and LOD models into coordinated, buildable solutions, developing geometry, detailing, and design intent through to production and as-built documentation.

  • Concept Development
  • Design Detailing
  • Production
  • As-Built
02 — BIM Delivery Coordinated project information

Lead BIM strategy, model quality, and multidisciplinary coordination, driving clash resolution, design reviews, and coordinated model delivery across project teams.

  • Model Management
  • Clash Resolution
  • Coordination
  • BIM Handover
03 — Digital Workflows Connected design and BIM

Integrate complex and parametric designs into Revit using Rhino, Grasshopper and Rhino.Inside.Revit. Coordinate model and geometry exchange with specialist tools to support design development and fabrication.

  • Rhino + Grasshopper
  • Rhino.Inside.Revit
  • Parametric Design
  • Interoperability
04 — Digital Fabrication Preparing geometry for physical production

Prepare and optimise digital geometry for 3D printing, scanning, CNC machining, and styrofoam fabrication, supporting the transition from coordinated models to production.

  • 3D Printing
  • 3D Scanning
  • CNC Machining
  • Styrofoam Fabrication
05 — Site Coordination Design intent verified on site

Support site teams by validating design intent against actual site conditions, resolving technical interfaces and coordinating updates to improve constructability and maintain alignment between the model and the built environment.

  • Site Verification
  • Technical Resolution
  • Constructability
  • Design Compliance

Project Description

SeaWorld Abu Dhabi on Yas Island is a next-generation marine life theme park featuring six immersive realms and the world’s largest aquarium with 68,000+ animals. Centered on the “One Ocean” theme, it showcases diverse habitats and inspires visitors to protect marine ecosystems.
